# v1 scope amendment — 2026-11-11

Authority: the user's explicit decision in the #4 chat. This amendment supersedes earlier requirements to ship local speech-to-text and pgvector in this version. Existing measurements and the source audit remain historical evidence, not revised results. No installer or app-source implementation is claimed by this planning update.

## Decisions

- **Remove local speech-to-text from v1.** Exclude whisper.cpp server, its tiny speech model and speech-only native resources from v1 installation/startup/readiness/acceptance. Audio/video upload or recording must not imply transcription availability. Preserve independent playback, PDF/editor/media and source/website history; wire an honest unavailable state for deferred transcription during implementation. No substitute cloud transcription service or fake transcript is introduced.
- **Keep PostgreSQL.** Bundle a private local PostgreSQL cluster, reviewed schema/roles/seeds and data lifecycle. No SQLite/database replacement is selected.
- **Defer pgvector.** Do not bundle/load the extension or require its vector type, cosine operator or HNSW index in the v1 bootstrap. The current schema/repository must be adapted before a PostgreSQL-only build can work. Preserve historical migration hashes; do not blindly apply or delete vector-dependent migrations or discard existing vector data. Retain a reviewed future extension/upgrade path as deferred work.
- **Retain the text-embedding investigation because retained features consume it.** The 479.729 MB MiniLM snapshot measured under #4 is a multilingual **text embedding** model, not the Whisper speech model. Its smaller-runtime/quantization experiment remains scoped to PDF and existing YouTube-transcript reference retrieval. No optimization or compatibility result is claimed yet. A provider transcript is already text and does not require local speech recognition.
- OS/CPU/installer, 250 MB installer/750 MB installed, startup/RAM, local-data/no-cloud, payments-deferred and LAN classroom decisions remain in force. This is an explicit feature-scope change, not a relaxed resource budget.
